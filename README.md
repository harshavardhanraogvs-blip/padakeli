# Telugu Padakeli

A daily Telugu crossword. Each cell holds one akshara, the grid is 10x10, and every word has a Telugu and an English hint.

Play it: https://harshavardhanraogvs-blip.github.io/padakeli/

## How it works
- A new puzzle opens each day. Players see the newest puzzle dated today (India time) or earlier.
- - Type with English letters (for example naa gives the Telugu letter) or with a Telugu keyboard. It works on laptops and phones.
  - - Hints are shown in Telugu, with an English option.
    - - When you finish, you see your time and the other players who finished, with their names and times. Only a name, the time taken, the number of cells filled and correct, the help used and whether the puzzle was finished are recorded.
